**KESSLER WORKS** — an original three.js tactical FPS. Two four-player teams fight short
rounds on a decommissioned night foundry: ROOKS carry a charge to site A or B and hold it
until it blows, WARDENS hold the works and defuse it. Between rounds you spend credits on
four weapons, armour and three grenades. Gunfire is positional: stopping and aiming pays,
firing on the move does not. Everything — map, art, names, sounds — is made for this entry,
and the game is plain ES modules that need no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts        # serves game/ and opens http://localhost:8080
```

Click to deploy, WASD to move, shift to walk, ctrl to crouch, LMB fire, RMB aim, R reload,
E plant/defuse, B buy menu, Tab scoreboard.
